This paper introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. The suite covers multiple levels from elementary school to PhD and multiple disciplines across STEM, humanities, and social sciences, providing a robust benchmark for evaluating Chinese language understanding and reasoning capabilities of foundation models.
