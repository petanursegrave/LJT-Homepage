This paper explores composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations. We demonstrate that parameter-efficient modules (like LoRA adapters) can be effectively combined and transferred using simple arithmetic operations, enabling efficient knowledge transfer and multi-task learning without full model retraining.
